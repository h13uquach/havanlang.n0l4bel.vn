'HVL' IS OUT NOW: 30 TRACKS, 30 ARTWORKS

Three years after 99%, MCK returns with 'HVL', a 30-track album built as a dialogue between the artist he is today and Hoang Long, his inner child.

A WORLD YOU CAN HEAR AND SEE

Every song comes with its own artwork, made together with director Phuong Vu and designer Trung Bao (Fustic.). Producers maiki and fujibynight shaped the sound across two halves: a quiet, reflective first part and a louder second half full of swagger.

PHYSICAL EDITIONS

The CD comes with a printed booklet of all 30 artworks. A numbered double-vinyl edition with gatefold sleeve is available for pre-order in the shop.

LIVE IN DECEMBER

The 'HVL' LIVE tour stops in Ho Chi Minh City, Ha Noi and Da Nang. VIP tickets include front-zone access and an exclusive merch pack.
