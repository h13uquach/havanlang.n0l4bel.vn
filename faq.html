FAQ

01 / N0L4B3LHow did you come up with the name N0L4B3L? What is the group's goal?

The name reads as 'NO LABEL' in stylised letters: a collective built to make music and visuals without waiting for anyone's permission. It brings together MCK, director Phuong Vu, Trung Bao (Fustic.), producers maiki and fujibynight, and other young creators. The goal is to turn an album into a whole world you can hear and see.

02 / STYLEWhat inspires your style of music?

MCK began in 2018 as the indie singer-songwriter Nger, moved into hip-hop in 2019 and broke out on Rap Viet season 1 in 2020. His style blends melodic rap with very personal storytelling: 99% (2023) turns 16 songs into 16 feelings about love, while HVL looks further inward.

03 / HVLWhat does the album name 'HVL' mean?

HVL is MCK's 30-track album, released in 2026, about three years after 99%. It is read as a dialogue between MCK today and Hoang Long, his inner child.

04 / ORDERSHow long does delivery take?

Orders in Ho Chi Minh City and Ha Noi arrive in 2-3 days, other provinces in 3-5 days. Shipping is a flat 30.000₫.

05 / TICKETSCan I get a refund on concert tickets?

No. Tickets cannot be refunded after purchase, but you can transfer them to a friend before the show by contacting us.
